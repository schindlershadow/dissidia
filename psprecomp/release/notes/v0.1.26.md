## Highlights

- **Online Tag Team Battle is much more stable.** Matches now stay in sync between PC, Steam Deck, Windows and Android, even when players run different frame rates (including Unlocked), switch targets, or play with full five-member teams.
- **Less input lag online.** A short slowdown on one device now pauses the match for a moment instead of adding delay for the rest of the fight. The ping notice shows your direct connection.
- **"Out of sync" names the player** it happened with.
- **Shaders are prepared at startup on every platform**, so fewer stutters in battle. If one still has to be built mid-fight, a small "Compiling shaders..." notice explains the hitch.
- **Vulkan is the default renderer on desktop**, with an automatic switch to OpenGL (and a notice) when Vulkan isn't available.
- **DLC works:** the game starts with DLC installed, DLC can be added as a mod, and it can be imported from PPSSPP.

## Tag Team Battle fixes
- Forfeiting or leaving an online match no longer leaves players stuck on a loading or gray screen.
- After a KO you can always deal damage again, and your BRV recovers (2v2 and Free-for-all).
- A KO no longer freezes a match with full five-member teams.
- Free-for-all: every player now gets their own tag-in.
- Forward and back stick moves work right after a tag-in.
- The partner panel keeps showing BREAK until that partner's BRV recovers.
- Lock-on markers stay visible after tag-ins; the battle format dialog no longer pops up every match.
- Players with different voice languages (Japanese / English) no longer go out of sync.

## Other fixes
- Japanese voices: fixed a silent fighter when HD texture packs are installed.
- The installer no longer freezes on the folder / file dialog.
- Windows: no Visual C++ Redistributable needed anymore.
- xdelta mods work again (the retail disc table is shipped again).
